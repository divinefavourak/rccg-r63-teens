# 3 — All the Screens

*(Plain-English version of `docs/04-information-architecture.md`. Read that file for the full detail.)*

This is the map of the whole product — every screen, grouped under 5 tabs.

---

## The big idea: 5 tabs, based on what teens want

Teens don't think "podcasts" or "articles". They arrive wanting something:

- *"I want my daily time with God"* → **Today**
- *"I want to read the Bible"* → **Bible**
- *"I want something to watch, listen to, or read"* → **Library**
- *"What's happening? What am I part of?"* → **Tribe**
- *"My progress, my saved things, my profile"* → **Me**

Those are the 5 tabs. Everything lives under one of them. The bottom nav never grows past 5.

Below, anything marked **(later)** is for a future version — don't design a finished screen for it. If a screen links to a "later" feature, just show a simple "Coming soon".

---

## 1. Today (the screen people land on)

- The day's header — date, a greeting, the streak flame
- Today's devotional (short reading) → opens the reading page
- The memory verse / verse of the day — this doubles as a share image
- Today's Bible chapter → opens the Bible
- One daily challenge (done right there)
- A "continue reading" card (pick up where you left off)

## 2. Bible (the heart of the app — centre tab)

- The reader (the main screen — book and chapter)
- The navigator (pick book → chapter → verse)
- Switch translation (WEB and KJV to start)
- Search the Bible
- Verse actions (share, save, copy) — highlights and notes are **(later)**
- My Bible — your bookmarks and history (highlights/notes **later**)
- Reading plans **(later)**

## 3. Library

- Browse — shelves like Featured, Series, Topics
- A category or series list
- The content itself — article reader, video player, or podcast player (with a mini-player that keeps playing)
- Search the library
- Manuals — only visible to teachers
- Journeys catalogue **(later)**

## 4. Tribe

- **Events** — upcoming list, event details, sign-up → payment → confirmation, my tickets (QR), past events
- **Announcements** — church notices
- **My Church** — your parish, service times, leaders
- **Community Notes (later)** — short testimonies teens post; other teens can like and repost them (counts shown). This needs the safety tools built first.
- Friends **(later)**
- Prayer wall **(later)**
- My group **(later)**
- Serve / volunteering **(later)**

## 5. Me

- Profile — your name, parish, role tag
- Progress — streak details, reading stats, history
- Saved — everything you kept (content + Bible bookmarks)
- My tickets (a shortcut; the main home is Tribe → Events)
- Notifications inbox
- Settings — account, notification preferences, appearance (theme + text size), data & offline, help
- Recognition / badges **(later)**

---

## The leader Console (a separate desktop tool)

Church leaders (teachers, coordinators, admins) get an extra tool, opened on purpose from Me. It is **not** mixed into the teen screens. It can be denser, like an admin tool:

- Overview — a dashboard for their area
- People — members, roles
- Content — plan and publish devotionals and library items (draft → review → publish)
- Events — create and manage, see sign-ups and payments, check people in
- Manuals — upload weekly lesson manuals
- Analytics — charts for their area
- Settings

Leaders still get the full teen app too — a leader has a streak like everyone else. The Console is just an extra place.

---

## Guests (people who haven't signed up)

Guests see the same 5 tabs — it's a preview of the real thing, not a separate advert site:

- **Today** — today's reading is fully readable; the streak card says "Sign up to start your streak"
- **Bible** — fully readable and searchable. The Bible is **never** locked.
- **Library** — browse everything; some things ask you to sign up to play
- **Events** — view events; tapping Register starts a quick sign-up
- **Me** — becomes the sign-up / log-in screen

Sign-up prompts only appear when an action really needs an account. Never a pop-up the moment the app opens.

---

## Two rules for the future

1. New content types (music, audio Bible…) become a **shelf in Library** — never a new tab.
2. New community features go into **Tribe** — never a new tab.

The 5 tabs stay 5 tabs, forever.
